# LANLink launch film: plan

- **Angle:** the PC is over there, your phone is right here, and nothing goes through anyone else's server.
- **Hook (first 2 s):** the real PC app ("Ready for your phone") and the real phone app side by side, with the line "Your PC is over there."
- **Highlights:**
  1. the compact keyboard built for a PC (Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Alt, arrows);
  2. pairing that a stray link cannot complete (the real "Pair with Studio PC?" screen);
  3. proof as real facts: hardware H.264, QUIC with TLS 1.3, the measured round trip, the test counts.
- **Punchline:** "No cloud. No account. Just your Wi-Fi."
- **Tone:** default (punchy, clean), in the apps' own palette: night #15181C, mist #E9E6DF, sandstone #D9B98C. Type: Inter.
- **Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, 21 s. Silent: no music source on this machine, and the README plays it muted.

## Stand-ins

All names and addresses are fictional: "Studio PC", 192.168.1.20, the PC identity shown, and "Pixel 9 Pro XL". The phone screenshots come from the Android emulator with that fictional data. The PC app screenshot is cropped to leave out the real computer name and address. The keyboard image is cropped from a real phone screenshot, with the desktop behind the keys removed.
